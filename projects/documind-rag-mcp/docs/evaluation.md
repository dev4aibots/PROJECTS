# Evaluation

Generated by `python evals/run.py` using deterministic local providers; no external LLM or credentials were used. The offline faithfulness judge checks that the answer claim is copied from retrieved context. This is reproducible regression evidence, not a substitute for a human or live-model judge.

| Metric | Score | Cases |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retrieval hit | 100% | 10 |
| Faithfulness (offline judge) | 100% | 15 |
| Citation accuracy | 100% | 15 |
| Refusal correctness | 100% | 5 |

## Dataset

15 authored cases over three fictional PDFs: 10 answerable questions with gold pages and 5 deliberately unanswerable questions.

## Limitations

Scores describe this frozen sample only. Live Gemini/Groq embeddings and generation require a separate credentialed evaluation before production claims.
