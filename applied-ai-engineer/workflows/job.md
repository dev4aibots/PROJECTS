# Job simulation mode

Use assessments/JOB_SIMULATIONS.md. Pick one task, declare timebox, environment, allowed tools, deliverables and scoring before starting. Initial independent reasoning must be separate from AI-assisted implementation. Include an unfamiliar codebase or changed requirements, not merely replaying memorized projects.

Score correctness, reasoning, security, simplicity, reliability, maintainability, communication, tradeoffs and independence from 0-5 with artifact citations. A critical auth/tenant/unsafe-action error blocks passing regardless of average. Scores are reviewer judgments, not automatically verified competence.

Ask for a technical plan, bounded implementation/PR, tests, observed results and written handoff. Inject one realistic change or outage after the first working slice. Review as a technical lead: what changed, why, what could break, how to reverse it and what evidence supports completion? Produce targeted remediation, not generic career praise.
